Quest rows in the panel now start with the quest level ("[38] Kill Shadowmaw Panther"), coloured by how hard it is for you. Each quest row has a small x and a right-click "Hide from plan"; hidden quests leave NOW, HERE, TRIPS and the map badges at once and wait under LATER, "Hidden by you", with an Unhide link (Settings, Plan, lists them with "Unhide all"). A new Plan page sets how many levels above you the plan reaches (default 3, or no limit) and whether elite quests are included. Settings, Map has opacity sliders for stop badges, badge labels, route lines, the line to your next stop, area rings and zones, and minimap pins, plus "Full opacity on hover".
